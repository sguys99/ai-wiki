# Better data. More capable robots.

We are building Cupel for robotics data and Tether Studio as the visual workspace for Tether. Prepare the data robots learn from, then develop, run, and improve their policies.

[Explore the products ↗](#products)[Talk to the founders →](mailto:team@fastcrest.com)

Our products

## Two products. Each useful on its own.

One helps you build the right dataset. The other helps you build a policy you understand and can improve.

01 / Robotics data

### Cupel

Turn demonstrations into data robots can learn from.

Start with robot recordings, human video, spatial data, or handheld demonstrations. Cupel's goal is to prepare a dataset for your robot, task, and training setup, measure whether it helps, and identify what data you need next.

- Understand, align, repair, and enrich source data
- Adapt demonstrations and compile target-specific datasets
- Evaluate training utility and plan the next collection

[Explore Cupel →](/cupel)[Follow an example workflow](/cupel/#example)

02 / Policy development

### Tether Studio

The visual workspace for Tether.

Bring Tether’s model preparation, serving, optimization, inspection, improvement, and release workflows into one interface. Follow a policy through an experiment with its settings and evidence in view.

- Prepare models and configure policy execution
- Inspect runs, optimize, and compare changes
- Carry checks and results into release decisions

[Explore Tether Studio →](/tether-studio)[Follow an example workflow](/tether-studio/#example)

Before you start

## A few practical questions.

Do I need both products?+

No. Cupel and Tether Studio are separate products. Use Cupel for your data workflow, or Tether Studio for policy development. Neither requires the other.

Do I need a physical robot?+

You can start with existing data in Cupel or supported local simulation workflows in Tether Studio. Checking a real deployment eventually requires evidence from the intended hardware.

How do I get started?+

Email [team@fastcrest.com](mailto:team@fastcrest.com) with the data or policy you have, the robot or task you are targeting, and the problem you want to solve.
